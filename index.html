Delete your Habi account and data

Habi — Daily Habit Tracker · Published by Habi Studio (Thanh Nguyen Xuan) · Last updated: October 6, 2026

Option 1 — Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Habi and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Delete Account.
  3. Confirm. Deletion is permanent and takes effect immediately.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you can't open the app, email thanhnguyenxuan970@gmail.com from the Google account you use in Habi, with the subject “Delete my Habi account”. We reply within 48 hours and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your habit data on our servers: habits, activity logs, streaks, challenges, achievements, rewards you set, and leaderboard progress.
  • The backup copy of your habit data and its history.
  • Your friend connections and your Habi profile.
  • Data stored on your device (removed when you delete the account in the app, or when you uninstall Habi).

What is kept, and for how long

  • Feedback you sent (your message, account email and device details) and your sign-in record (email address and name) are not removed automatically by the in-app deletion. Email us and we will delete them within 30 days.
  • Crash reports and screen recordings sent to our crash-reporting provider (Sentry) are deleted automatically within 30 days. They do not include your email address.

More details: Habi Privacy Policy.
