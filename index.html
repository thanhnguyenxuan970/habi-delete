Request Account Deletion

All your Habi data (habits, activity logs, streaks) is stored locally on your device. No account exists on our servers.

Data we hold:

• Your Google profile (name, email, avatar) — stored only on your device in secure storage.

• All habit and activity data — stored only on your device in a local database.

• We do not retain any personal data on our servers.

To delete your data

  • 1 Open Settings on your Android device.
  • 2 Go to Apps → Habi.
  • 3 Tap Storage → Clear Data — this deletes all local habit data and signs you out.
  • 4 Optionally, uninstall Habi to remove all app files.
